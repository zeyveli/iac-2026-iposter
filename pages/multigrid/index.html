Matrix-free BiCGSTAB–GMG

The pressure-correction operator is evaluated as compact gradient then divergence, DₕGₕ, rather than stored as a global matrix. BiCGSTAB uses GMG as its geometric preconditioner.

GMG preconditioner: geometric hierarchy and W-cycle

Preconditioner: second-order seven-point Laplacian. Restriction uses full weighting; prolongation is linear. These inexpensive transfers connect successively coarser geometric levels.

Smoother: wall-normal line relaxation. It reuses the same batched-tridiagonal infrastructure developed for compact line solves, now applied on every GMG level.

The compact DₕGₕ operator remains matrix-free inside BiCGSTAB. The seven-point Laplacian is a separate, deliberately low-cost GMG preconditioner—not a replacement for the compact pressure operator.
