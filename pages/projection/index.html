Store, predict, project, return

Velocity components live at nodes while pressure is cell-centred. Divergence and pressure-gradient operations are evaluated at staggered face-normal locations before the corrected velocity is returned to the nodal representation.

The matrix-free compact pressure operator is applied as DhGh. The face-normal velocity is corrected by the pressure increment; compact interpolation then returns a nodal representation for the following timestep.
